Professional Summary

Musicologist working at the intersection of empirical performance studies, music historiography and music education. Doctoral research on tempo, performance time and portamento in Beethoven’s piano and cello sonatas has yielded five preprints (2025–2026), a 346-page thesis, and an open dataset and analysis code in Python and MATLAB. Ph.D. self-funded through over five years of senior music leadership in UK independent schools.

Research Experience

  • 2021 - 2025

    Aberdeen, United Kingdom

    Independent Doctoral Researcher
    University of Aberdeen
    Supervisor: Dr Jonathan Hicks.
    • Designed and executed a multi-year empirical research programme on tempo, performance time and portamento in Beethoven’s five piano and cello sonatas — from research questions through methodology, data collection, analysis and writing.
    • Assembled, digitised and annotated a corpus of over 100 commercial and archival recordings spanning 1930–2012 (Casals, Feuermann, Fournier, Piatigorsky, du Pré, Rostropovich, Shafran, Starker, Tortelier, Ma, Wispelwey, Bylsma, Maisky, Perényi, Kliegel, Isserlis and others).
    • Developed an original manual bar-by-bar tempo-measurement protocol, characterised to millisecond resolution, after documenting the failure of automated beat-detection tools on polyphonic duo recordings.
    • Implemented k-means clustering (scikit-learn, k-means++, 100 restarts) identifying three coexisting tempo traditions stable across eight decades; developed a spline-CDF smoothing method for histogram density estimation.
    • Produced a five-tool visualisation suite (tempographs, histograms, ridgeline plots, stacked bar charts, combination charts) in Python and MATLAB, openly published on GitHub.
  • 2021 - 2025

    Stockholm, Sweden

    Cross-Disciplinary Collaboration
    KTH Royal Institute of Technology
    • Collaborated with an engineer (VLSI design) on the mathematical framework, error model and data architecture of the tempo-measurement protocol.
    • Built an open-access reproducible research pipeline in Python (pandas, numpy, scipy, matplotlib, seaborn, scikit-learn) and MATLAB.
