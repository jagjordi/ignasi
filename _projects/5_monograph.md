My immediate priority is the publication of the doctoral work **in book form**. The monograph will incorporate the five preprints as revised chapters and add substantial new material on Beethoven's historical pianos, the cultural history of portamento, and the implications of the ecological model of stylistic change for performance studies as a discipline.

A proposal will be submitted to a major university press within the next year. **Target submission: 2026–2027.**
