I intend to extend the methodology to Beethoven's **violin sonatas and selected string quartets**. The violin sonatas are an ideal test case: their recorded tradition is richer than the cello sonatas', and violin technique and portamento history diverge from cello practice in instructive ways.

A full k-means clustering analysis would test whether the three-tradition structure documented in the cello works **generalises across Beethoven's chamber output**, or whether it is specific to the cello–piano duo. Preliminary work can begin immediately, with one or two papers anticipated within two years.
