Applying **k-means clustering** ($$k=3$$, k-means++ initialisation, 100 restarts) to bar-level BPM data from more than one hundred movement-level recordings, I found that every movement of the five sonatas supports at least two — usually three — discrete tempo traditions whose internal regression slopes are negligible across 1930–2012.

What changes across eight decades is the *relative prevalence* of coexisting traditions, not a uniform drift toward a new norm. I call this the **ecological model of stylistic change** {% cite sole2026kmeans %}.
