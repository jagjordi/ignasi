Automated beat-detection tools fail systematically on historical duo recordings. In response I developed a formalised **manual bar-by-bar tempo-measurement protocol** built on a cumulative timestamp architecture that prevents error accumulation, permits internal self-validation, and captures the expressive timing phenomena — rubato, fermatas, ritardandi — that automated tools suppress or misread.

The mathematical derivation, spreadsheet data structure and full error characterisation were developed in cross-disciplinary collaboration with an engineer specialising in VLSI design {% cite sole2026protocol %}.
